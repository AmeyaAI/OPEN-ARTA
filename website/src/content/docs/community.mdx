## How decisions get made

The project's review policies are written down and enforced: the local
single-team loop stays free forever, truthful reporting beats flattering
reporting, and no customer-specific defaults land in core. If a PR
contradicts one of those, it doesn't merge — including ours. Read the
[contributing guide](/docs/contributing/) before your first PR;
a lightweight CLA is signed with a single PR comment.
